This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect user information when you use our game.

Device information (device model, operating system version)
Game progress and gameplay statistics
Crash reports and diagnostics

We do not collect personal information such as:

Real names
Addresses
Phone numbers
Payment card information

unless explicitly provided by the user through external services.

Google AdMob

3. Analytics and Diagnostics

We may use analytics and crash reporting tools to improve gameplay experience, performance, and stability.

Session duration
Device type
Crash logs
Gameplay interactions

Rubix is not directed specifically to children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.
